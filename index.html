04 — RESEARCH & PUBLICATIONS

Evidence, research & impact.

Research work exploring the contribution of clinical pharmacy interventions to medication safety and patient care.

Published Research

Clinical Outcomes of Pharmacist Interventions in Critical Care

Clinical Research Riyadh, Saudi Arabia Six-month study

A six-month cross-sectional study evaluating clinical and patient outcomes associated with pharmacist interventions in a critical care setting at a private hospital in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia.

1,222 Pharmacist Interventions
1,124 Adult Patients
30 Critical Care Beds
6 mo. Study Period

Selected Findings

49.9% of interventions were classified as potentially significant.
29.5% were classified as potentially serious interventions.
25.9% involved anti-infective medications.
33.4% were associated with improvement in the patient's condition.
10.9% reached a therapeutic endpoint.
